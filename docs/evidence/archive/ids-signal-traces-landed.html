§1 — a pulse's signals read as traces from its IDS

imas_ink/extract.py gained extract_signal_traces(ids, path), exported beside extract_time_traces. It walks an unindexed data-dictionary path, expanding every struct array on the way, and returns one TimeSeries per element whose data is filled. Each series takes its time from ids.time under homogeneous time 1 and from the node's own time under 0. Its unit comes from node.data.metadata.units, and its y-label from the element's non-empty name, falling back to the path's leading component. No series carries a legend label. A path holding an index, and homogeneous time 2, raise ValueError. Nothing is scaled, filtered or resampled.

tests/unit/ink/test_signal_traces.py builds IDS in memory with imas.IDSFactory. It covers a three-coil pf_active with one unfed coil, which yields two series in A; a magnetics ip; flux loops under homogeneous time 0, each with its own flux.time in Wb; and both refusals.
